CBN pushes market-led liquidity management with repo, swap markets

BusinessDay · Sat 28 Nov, 13:10 Lagos

What happened

The Central Bank of Nigeria plans to introduce interbank repo and overnight indexed swap markets in the first quarter of 2027 to deepen money markets and reduce reliance on the apex bank. Governor Olayemi Cardoso announced the plan at the 61st Annual Bankers' Dinner of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria in Lagos.

Key facts

  • CBN plans to launch interbank repo and overnight indexed swap markets in the first quarter of 2027.
  • Olayemi Cardoso, CBN governor, announced the plan at the 61st Annual Bankers' Dinner in Lagos.
  • Daily fixed-income market turnover averages N1.5 trillion, according to Cardoso.
  • Foreign exchange market turnover has at times exceeded $1 billion daily.

Named: Central Bank of Nigeria, Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria, Olayemi Cardoso

4 · Regulation & enforcement · Policy

PANDEF opposes 20% cut from local content fund for new research agency

Premium Times · Sat 28 Nov, 05:22 Lagos

What happened

PANDEF has urged National Assembly members to reconsider a funding provision in a bill to establish the National Council on Research, Science, Technology and Innovation. The bill proposes deducting 20 per cent of the Nigerian Content Development Fund as a primary funding source for the council.

Key facts

  • Section 19(a) of the bill proposes deducting 20 per cent of the Nigerian Content Development Fund (NCDF) for the proposed research council.
  • The bill was read on the Senate floor on 13 October, passed second reading on 22 October, and scaled third reading on 19 November.
  • The bill is currently at the second reading stage in the House of Representatives.
  • Godknows Igali, national chairman of PANDEF, made the call at a press briefing in Abuja on Friday.

Named: Pan Niger Delta Forum (PANDEF), Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board (NCDMB), Godknows Igali, Opeyemi Bamidele

5 · Ownership & capital · Restructuring

Oando closes at N39.90 after full-bid demand on rights issue announcement day

Nairametrics · Fri 27 Nov, 19:21 Lagos

What happened

Oando Plc closed at N39.90 per share on Friday, November 27, 2026, up 9.92% on the day, after announcing the final terms of its N203.8 billion rights issue. The company is offering 4.076 billion new ordinary shares at N50 per share, one new share for every two held by qualifying shareholders.

Key facts

  • Oando closed at N39.90 per share on Friday, November 27, 2026, gaining N3.60 or 9.92% from N36.30.
  • Oando announced the final terms of its N203.8 billion rights issue on Friday following regulatory approvals.
  • The rights issue offers 4.076 billion new ordinary shares at N50 per share, one new share for every two existing shares.
  • Investors traded 17,364,939 Oando shares worth N691.87 million across 491 deals on Friday.

AURA analysis · interpretation, not reported fact

Why it matters: The rights issue would increase Oando's share capital and shareholding base, and the proceeds are intended for upstream oil and gas investment, working capital and debt repayment. Shareholders face a gap between the N50 subscription price and the N39.90 market close when deciding whether to subscribe.

Named: Oando Plc
