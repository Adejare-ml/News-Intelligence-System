Sunbeth completes N19.5 billion acquisition of UAC’s 73.29% stake in Livestock Feeds, reconstitutes Board

Nairametrics · Tue 17 Nov, 14:24 Lagos

What happened

Sunbeth Treenuts and Sesame Limited has completed its N19.5 billion all-cash acquisition of UAC of Nigeria Plc's 73.29% stake in Livestock Feeds Plc. The transaction followed approvals from the FCCPC, SEC and NGX, and the company has reconstituted its board with three resignations and five new directors.

Key facts

  • Sunbeth Treenuts and Sesame Limited acquired UAC of Nigeria Plc's 73.29% stake in Livestock Feeds Plc for N19.5 billion, all cash.
  • The transaction was announced on September 30, 2026, at an implied price of N8.85 per Livestock Feeds share.
  • Sam Okuwlehi, Seyi Ebenezer, Professor Franklin Nnaemeka Ngwu, Mary Mulili and Farrukh Siddiqui were appointed directors on November 17, 2026.

AURA analysis · interpretation, not reported fact

Why it matters: The change of majority ownership and full board turnover at Livestock Feeds shifts control of a listed Nigerian animal feed company to Sunbeth, and the article names Sunbeth as the buyer without disclosing its beneficial owners.

Ownership details as reported by Nairametrics; not verified with CAC records.

Named: Livestock Feeds Plc, Sunbeth Treenuts and Sesame Limited, UAC of Nigeria Plc, Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission, Securities and Exchange Commission

Billionaire Tony Elumelu adds N98 billion stake in Seplat

Nairametrics · Tue 17 Nov, 09:48 Lagos

What happened

Heirs Energies, the energy investment company of Tony Elumelu, bought an additional 5,888,341 Seplat Energy Plc shares at a cost of approximately N98 billion. The purchase lifts the Heirs Holdings Group's stake in Seplat to 22.05%, a holding the article values at approximately US$1.6 billion. Elumelu is also set to become Seplat's chairman effective January 1, 2027.

Key facts

  • Heirs Energies acquired 5,888,341 additional Seplat Energy Plc shares at approximately N98 billion.
  • The latest purchase brings the Heirs Holdings Group's total Seplat shareholding to 22.05%.
  • The Group's total Seplat holding is valued at approximately US$1.6 billion.
  • Elumelu is set to become Seplat Chairman effective January 1, 2027, succeeding Senator Udoma Udo Udoma.

Ownership details as reported by Nairametrics; not verified with CAC records.

Named: Heirs Energies, Heirs Holdings, Seplat Energy Plc, Maurel & Prom, African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank)
