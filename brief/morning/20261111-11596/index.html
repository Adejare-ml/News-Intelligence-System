N210tn audit queries: Kyari finally appears before Senate panel

BusinessDay · Tue 10 Nov, 20:13 Lagos · latest of 3 reports from 3 outlets

What happened

Mele Kyari, former Group Chief Executive Officer of NNPC Limited, appeared before the Senate Committee on Public Accounts to answer questions over N210 trillion in financial transactions flagged in the company's 2017–2023 accounts.

Key facts

  • Mele Kyari appeared before the Senate Committee on Public Accounts on Tuesday.
  • The N210 trillion comprises N103 trillion accrued expenses and N107 trillion sundry receivables.
  • The committee is chaired by Senator Ibrahim Dankwambo, representing Gombe North.
  • Ex-NNPCL boss Kyari appeared before a Senate panel. (The Eagle Online)

LG autonomy: EFCC probes 774 chairmen’s spending

Punch · Tue 10 Nov, 00:50 Lagos

What happened

The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission is secretly investigating the 774 local government chairmen across Nigeria over their management and spending of public funds since the councils gained financial autonomy, according to The PUNCH. Sources say the probe is being handled by EFCC zonal directorates across the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory.

Key facts

  • The EFCC is investigating the 774 local government chairmen over spending since councils gained financial autonomy.
  • The investigation is being handled by the commission's zonal directorates.
  • On July 11, 2024, the Supreme Court ruled local government financial autonomy unconstitutionally blocked by state governors.
