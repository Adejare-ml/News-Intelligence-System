BREAKING: NERC approves N30 billion to provide free meters to Band A, Band B customers

Nairametrics · Tue 10 Nov, 15:18 Lagos

What happened

The Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) has approved N30 billion for the third tranche of the Meter Acquisition Fund (MAF) to provide free electricity meters to unmetered Band A customers and accelerate metering for Band B customers. Under Order No. NERC/2026/121, the tranche takes effect from November 2, 2026, with all installations to be completed by May 31, 2027.

Key facts

  • Funds will be distributed among the 11 electricity distribution companies (DisCos) according to their contributions to the Fund.

AURA analysis · interpretation, not reported fact

Why it matters: The programme commits N30 billion of public-linked funds to meter procurement across 11 DisCos, with contracts required to be filed with NERC, which strengthens oversight of how the money is spent.

5 · Public money · Procurement

AURA risk rating: Medium · 35/100

DisCos get N30bn as NERC disburse third tranch of meter acquisition fund

BusinessDay · Tue 10 Nov, 15:50 Lagos

What happened

The Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) has approved N30 billion as the third tranche of the Meter Acquisition Fund, to be allocated among eleven Distribution Companies (DisCos) in proportion to their contributions. Under Order No. NERC/2026/121, DisCos must run transparent procurement involving eligible Local Meter Manufacturers and Assemblers and Meter Asset Providers, with installations due by 31 May 2027.

Key facts

  • NERC approved N30 billion as the third tranche of the Meter Acquisition Fund.
  • The fund is allocated among eleven DisCos in proportion to their contributions to the Fund.
  • Payment to selected providers is made 70 percent on verified delivery and 30 percent on complete installation.
  • All meter installations under Tranche C must be completed on or before 31 May 2027.

AURA analysis · interpretation, not reported fact

Why it matters: The order sets procurement, pricing and reporting rules for public metering funds, and requires all supply and installation contracts to be filed with the Commission, which supports transparency over how DisCos spend the money.

Named: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission, Meter Acquisition Fund
