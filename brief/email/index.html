Duke Oil: NNPC explains $2bn crude-for-petrol transactions

Punch · Wed 25 Nov, 00:37 Lagos

What happened

NNPC Limited has denied claims that $2.04bn in crude oil proceeds linked to its trading subsidiary, Duke Oil Company Incorporated, now NNPC Trading S.A., is missing, stating all Direct Sale Direct Purchase transactions were fully accounted for.

Key facts

  • NNPC says the $2.04bn represented the gross value of 29 crude cargoes and was not missing or unpaid revenue.
  • NNPC states 30 cargoes were paid for in dollars and proceeds from 28 cargoes, valued at approximately $1.96bn, were remitted in naira.
  • The remaining cargo, a February 2023 Bonga tax-oil lifting valued at approximately $75.9m, was paid into the Federal Inland Revenue Service dollar account.
  • Duke Oil was incorporated in 1989 as a wholly owned trading subsidiary of NNPC, according to the company.

Named: Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited, Duke Oil Company Incorporated, NNPC Trading S.A., Federal Inland Revenue Service, Andy Odeh

Coronation Insurance advances court-sanction scheme at ₦2.88 per share

Nairametrics · Thu 26 Nov, 09:41 Lagos

What happened

Coronation Insurance Plc has announced progress on its Scheme of Arrangement after SEC approval and Federal High Court sanction, setting a Final Scheme Consideration of ₦2.88 per share. Core Shareholders have deposited the consideration into escrow, and Coronation Registrars Limited is expected to begin disbursement shortly. The Company is expected to be delisted from the NGX after implementation.

Key facts

  • Final Scheme Consideration is ₦2.88 per share, a 336.4% increase over the ₦0.66 offer proposed in 2021.
  • Scheme resolutions received 97.88% support at the Court-Ordered Meeting on 22 December 2023.
  • SEC approval was granted on 22 October 2026; the Federal High Court sanctioned the Scheme on 10 November 2026.
  • Core Shareholders have deposited the Scheme Consideration funds into escrow in accordance with the Scheme Document.
