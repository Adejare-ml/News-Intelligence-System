Dockworkers’ service conditions undergo review

Punch · Thu 26 Nov, 01:06 Lagos

What happened

NIMASA and the Maritime Workers' Union of Nigeria have begun reviewing minimum standards and conditions of service for dockworkers for 2026–2028. Proposals from employers, unions and industry groups were presented at a reconvened National Joint Industrial Council meeting. Further consultations are planned towards a harmonised framework for the dock labour industry.

Key facts

  • NIMASA and the Maritime Workers' Union of Nigeria commenced a review of dockworkers' minimum standards and conditions of service for 2026–2028.
  • Submissions cover wages, workers' welfare, occupational safety and health, pension provisions, working conditions, protective equipment and skills development.
  • Osagie Edward, Deputy Director/Head of Public Relations at NIMASA, issued the statement on Wednesday.
  • Jibril Abba, Executive Director, Maritime Labour and Cabotage Services at NIMASA, chairs the NJIC.
