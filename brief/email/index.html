Sunbeth completes acquisition of controlling stake in Livestock Feeds Plc

Premium Times · Wed 18 Nov, 15:36 Lagos

What happened

Sunbeth Treenuts and Sesame Limited, a subsidiary of Sunbeth Agro HoldCo, has completed its acquisition of UAC of Nigeria Plc's 73.29% shareholding in Livestock Feeds Plc. The completion followed approvals from the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission, the Securities and Exchange Commission and Nigerian Exchange Limited.

Key facts

  • Sunbeth Treenuts and Sesame Limited acquired UAC of Nigeria Plc's 73.29% shareholding in Livestock Feeds Plc.
  • Approvals came from the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission, the Securities and Exchange Commission and Nigerian Exchange Limited.
  • Peeyush Karnani is Chief Strategy Officer of Sunbeth Agro HoldCo.
