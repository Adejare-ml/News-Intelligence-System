Coca-Cola HBC completes $2.6 billion acquisition of African bottler CCBA

Nairametrics · Fri 27 Nov, 13:18 Lagos

What happened

Coca-Cola HBC has completed its $2.6 billion acquisition of Coca-Cola Beverages Africa (CCBA), following regulatory approval from Tanzania's Fair Competition Commission. The deal gives Coca-Cola HBC control of a bottling business operating in 14 African countries and makes it the world's second-largest Coca-Cola bottler by volume.

Key facts

  • Coca-Cola HBC acquired a 75% stake in CCBA from The Coca-Cola Company and Gutsche Family Investments for $2.6 billion.
  • Coca-Cola HBC issued 21,027,676 new ordinary shares to Gutsche Family Investments, representing 5.45% of its enlarged share capital.
  • CCBA recorded 2025 revenue of $3.69 billion, up 1.6% from $3.63 billion in 2024.
  • Coca-Cola HBC has reaffirmed plans to invest up to $1 billion in Nigeria over five years.

AURA analysis · interpretation, not reported fact

Why it matters: The acquisition expands a major multinational bottler's operations across Africa, including Nigeria, and its planned Nigerian investment affects manufacturing capacity and employment in the country.

Named: Coca-Cola HBC, Coca-Cola Beverages Africa, The Coca-Cola Company, Gutsche Family Investments, Nigerian Bottling Company
