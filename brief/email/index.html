Top NGX-listed tech companies ranked by revenue growth in 2026

Nairametrics · Sun 8 Nov, 16:05 Lagos

What happened

Revenue growth across nine NGX-listed technology and telecommunications companies was mixed in 2026, according to an analysis of their latest financial statements as of November 8, 2026. Omatek Ventures recorded the highest growth at 650% from a very low base, while Legend Internet posted the weakest performance with a 7.4% revenue decline. Several companies reported rising revenue alongside weaker profitability.

Key facts

  • Omatek Ventures Plc revenue rose 650% to approximately N1.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
  • Legend Internet revenue fell 7.4% to N1.10 billion for the 12 months ended July 31, 2026.
  • MTN Nigeria revenue grew 23.6% to N4.61 trillion for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
  • eTranzact profit after tax fell 42.7% to N1.38 billion from N2.41 billion.
