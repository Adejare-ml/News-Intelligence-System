Senate summons NCC, MTN, Airtel, others over poor network, unjustified airtime deductions

BusinessDay · Wed 11 Nov, 22:03 Lagos

What happened

The Nigerian Senate has summoned the Nigerian Communications Commission and major telecom operators, including MTN, Airtel, Globacom and T2, over persistent network failures and alleged unjustified airtime deductions. A Senate Committee on Communications, chaired by Ikra Bilbis, has been mandated to investigate subscriber losses and report back within four weeks.

Key facts

  • The motion was sponsored by Tahir Monguno (APC, Borno North), the Senate Chief Whip.
