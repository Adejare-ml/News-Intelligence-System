Zamfara among eight states that fully cleared inherited pension backlogs – PenCom report

Premium Times · Mon 16 Nov, 18:16 Lagos

What happened

On 10 November 2026 PenCom announced that Zamfara State had fully met its inherited pension obligations, joining seven other states that cleared similar backlogs. Governor Dauda Lawal credited President Tinubu’s fiscal reforms for the state’s improved capacity to settle retirees’ dues.

Key facts

  • Eight states cleared legacy pension backlogs: Lagos, Gombe, Borno, Anambra, Jigawa, Katsina, Kebbi, Zamfara.
  • Six states paid off more than 80% of pension liabilities: Delta, Osun, Ogun, Akwa Ibom, Kaduna, Oyo.
  • 36 states owed a combined pension liability exceeding N2 trillion before reforms.
