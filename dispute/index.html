Right of reply

Dispute a record

If AURA names you, or a company you act for, and something is wrong, tell us here. Every dispute is read by a person.

We reply here. It is never published.

Who are you in relation to the record?

Up to 4,000 characters. A link to a source (a CAC filing, a company statement) helps.

What happens next

We check the record against its source article and reply by email. If the record is wrong, we correct it or take it off the site, and log the change on the corrections page. While we look into it, the record may stay up.

Records on AURA are drawn from news reports, not from the CAC register. A record says that a news outlet reported something, not that it is true. See the terms and how we handle what you send in the privacy notice.

AURA reads Nigeria's business and regulatory news four times a day and maps who controls what.

Records are extracted by a language model from news reporting. They are not the statutory CAC register, and a record here is evidence of reporting, not a finding of wrongdoing. Names may be shared by more than one person or company. Everything here is machine-extracted and unverified.Dispute a record or ask for its removal.
