New to AURA? What you are looking at

What AURA is
AURA reads Nigerian business and regulatory news four times a day and pulls out who is reported to own or control which companies, board changes and public contracts.
What a PSC is
A person with significant control: anyone with at least 5% of a company's shares or votes, or who can otherwise direct it. The Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020 makes companies disclose them to the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC).
Where records come from
Every record is a claim made in a press report, linked to the article. None is a CAC filing, and being named is not a finding of wrongdoing.

    1. 01

      EFCC local government probe

      The EFCC is reportedly investigating the spending of all 774 local government chairmen since councils gained financial autonomy. Zonal directorates across the 36 states and the FCT are handling the work, and the EFCC spokesman did not respond to the reporter. The probe follows the Supreme Court's 11 July 2024 ruling on LG financial autonomy.

      The Punch
    2. 02

      Mompha case struck out

      The Lagos State Special Offences Court in Ikeja struck out the EFCC's N6bn money laundering charge against Ismaila Mustapha (Mompha) and Ismalob Global Investment Limited. Judge Mojisola Dada held that prosecuting while a related Federal High Court case is pending was an abuse of process. That case, over about N32.9bn, remains pending.
